Privacy Policy

CarnaticTranslate · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CarnaticTranslate ("the app," "we," "us") handles your information. CarnaticTranslate is developed and operated by Ananth Ram Tekkalakota. The app helps you transcribe Carnatic music notation from photographs of music sheets into editable, exportable documents.

The short version

CarnaticTranslate does not require an account, does not collect personal information for our own use, and stores your documents locally on your device unless you choose to turn on iCloud sync. When you start a scan, the image is sent through a proxy server we operate to the selected third-party AI provider. The proxy does not store the image. We log limited request metadata, but never images, prompts, or content.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app. For scan requests, we log limited metadata: request counts, the model used, token counts, and timing information. We never log or store your images, prompts, or content.

Images you scan (proxy and third-party AI processing)

When you start a scan, the app sends the image you capture or select through a proxy server we operate. The proxy forwards the request to a third-party artificial-intelligence provider — either Google (Gemini API) or OpenAI, depending on the model selected for that scan — and returns the transcription to the app. The proxy does not store your image, prompt, or content. It logs only the request metadata described above.

Your use of these providers is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API Terms & Privacy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

All scans use access to the AI providers supplied by the app.

Documents and where they are stored

Transcriptions you save — including the notation, any metadata you enter (such as title, ragam, talam, and composer), the associated page images, and generated PDFs — are stored locally on your device. We do not upload, sync, or back up your documents to any server we control. If you delete the app, locally stored content may be removed with it.

Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. If you enable it, your documents and associated page images sync through your own iCloud account. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy, and is not sent to or stored on any server we operate.

Children's privacy

CarnaticTranslate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Accuracy notice

Transcriptions are generated by AI and may contain errors. Always review and verify output against the original source before relying on it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, practices, or legal obligations change. We will update the effective date and provide prominent notice of material changes through the app or another appropriate channel. Where required, we will obtain your consent before applying a change. Updating this policy alone does not authorize new uses of previously collected information that require additional notice or consent.
