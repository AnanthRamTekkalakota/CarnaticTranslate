Privacy Policy

CarnaticTranslate · Last updated: 13 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CarnaticTranslate ("the app," "we," "us") handles your information. CarnaticTranslate is developed and operated by Ananth Ram Tekkalakota. The app helps you transcribe Carnatic music notation from photographs of music sheets into editable, exportable documents.

The short version

CarnaticTranslate does not require an account, does not collect personal information for our own use, and stores your documents locally on your device. The only data that leaves your device is the image you choose to scan, which is sent to a third-party AI provider solely to transcribe it. That's it.

Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app. We have no servers that store your content.

Images you scan (third-party AI processing)

To transcribe a music sheet, the app sends the image you capture or select to a third-party artificial-intelligence provider — either Google (Gemini API) or OpenAI, depending on the model selected for that scan. The provider processes the image and returns the transcribed notation. This transmission happens only when you start a scan.

Your use of these providers is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API Terms & Privacy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

Important distinction: When a scan uses the app's built-in (bundled) access to a provider, that provider may use the submitted data to improve their services, in accordance with their free-tier terms. When you supply your own API key ("Bring Your Own Key"), your scans are governed by your own account's terms with that provider. If you are concerned about how your images are used, review the provider's policy above and consider using your own key.

Documents and where they are stored

Transcriptions you save — including the notation, any metadata you enter (such as title, ragam, talam, and composer), the associated page images, and generated PDFs — are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, sync, or back up your documents to any server we control. If you delete the app, this locally stored content is removed with it.

API keys

If you choose to enter your own provider API key, it is stored securely in your device's Keychain and is used only to authenticate your scans with that provider. We never transmit your key to any server we operate.

Children's privacy

CarnaticTranslate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Accuracy notice

Transcriptions are generated by AI and may contain errors. Always review and verify output against the original source before relying on it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
