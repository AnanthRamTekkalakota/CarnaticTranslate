The workflow

A starting point for your next rendition.

Let AI help with transcription. Keep your musical judgment at the center.

  1. Bring in your pages

    Use the camera, choose photos, or import images and multi-page PDFs. Selected pages are sent for cloud AI processing when you transcribe.

  2. Review and organize

    Check the notation against your source. For a scan containing several songs, review the detected groups, adjust their page ranges, merge or split, and save songs separately.

  3. Make it useful

    Correct cells, refine the song’s details, and save the document with its source pages. Export a PDF for sharing or printing, or a .carnatic file to reopen in the app.

Before you begin

A few practical details.

Can I rely on the transcription as-is?

AI can misread notes, lyrics, structure and metadata. Compare the result with your source and correct it before using, sharing or printing it. Page accounting measures a technically usable result, not musical accuracy.

Do I need iCloud?

No. Save documents locally with sync off. If you choose iCloud, document JSON and source images sync through Apple. Turning it off does not remove cloud copies. Keep any additional backups you need.

Who is the service for?

The intended service minimum is 13. If you are under 18, you need a parent or legal guardian’s permission. Local age and consent rules also apply. This service requirement is separate from Apple’s content age rating. Read the eligibility terms.

How do deletion and cancellation work?

Document deletion, requests about server data, and Apple subscription cancellation are separate. There is currently no authenticated in-app server-data deletion workflow. Read the deletion limits or Apple’s cancellation instructions.
