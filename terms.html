Plans, page allowances, and service limits

The service’s plan status and issued allowance determine access in beta versions using server page accounting. Each page in a multi-page import is accounted for individually. An import or detected song is not a single page for accounting purposes. Existing allowance periods keep their issued limits until expiry; unused pages do not roll over, and a selected paid allowance replaces rather than stacks with Free.

The planned public-release allowances are 3 Pages per newly issued Free monthly anniversary period, 50 Pages for Standard and 100 Pages for Plus per paid monthly allowance period. Pricing is coming soon. These planned tiers are not an offer of a currently available public subscription. The current allowance shown by the service governs your beta use.

Where page accounting and response recovery are enabled, the service reserves a page in progress and settles it for a technically usable result. Partial success settles only the pages that produce usable results. Technical usability is not musical accuracy. A terminal provider or validation failure before settlement releases that page reservation; technical rate limits can still count the request. A matching lost-response recovery within the available retrieval window returns the prior result without a second provider call or page charge.

We may apply request-size, rate, concurrency and processing-time limits to protect the service. Recovery, sync, exports and beta availability are not guaranteed. Page packs are not offered.
