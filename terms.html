What the app does

CarnaticTranslate lets you photograph or import Carnatic music notation sheets and uses our Worker and third-party AI services to transcribe them into editable, exportable documents. The provider and model may be selected by the service configuration rather than by you. The intended release providers are Anthropic and OpenAI; production routing for that release is not yet confirmed. Existing and historical processing paths are described in the Privacy Policy. In builds that expose a custom compatible endpoint, its operator may be someone other than OpenAI.

Plans, page allowances, and service limits

The intended allowances are 3 pages for each newly issued Free monthly anniversary period, 50 for Standard and 100 for Plus per paid monthly allowance period. Existing allowance periods retain their issued limits until expiry. Page packs are not currently offered. Available plans and their current page allowances are shown in the app. The Worker is the authoritative source for your plan, allowance period, used pages, pages in progress, and remaining pages. We may apply reasonable technical limits to protect the service, including request-size, rate, concurrency, and processing-time limits.

The service reserves a page while a scan is in progress and settles that page when it produces a technically usable transcription. Each submitted page in a multi-page import is admitted and accounted for individually, so partial success can settle only the pages that produce usable results. A technically usable result can still contain transcription errors; page settlement is not an accuracy guarantee. A terminal provider or validation failure before settlement releases the plan-page reservation, although the request may still count toward technical rate limits. A matching lost-response recovery within the available retrieval window returns the prior result without a second provider call or page charge. Unused pages do not roll over between allowance periods or grants. A paid allowance replaces the Free allowance while the paid grant is selected; the two allowances do not stack.
